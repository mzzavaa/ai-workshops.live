How It Works

From challenge to clarity in four steps

A structured process that combines the speed of AI with the judgment of an experienced consultant.

1

Tell us your challenge

Your team fills a structured intake form covering your business context, current processes, and AI goals. Takes about 20 minutes.

2

AI agents analyze your situation

Specialized agents run use case scoring, technical feasibility checks, cost modeling, and architecture recommendations against your inputs.

3

Review session with an expert

A 30-minute video call to walk through the agent findings, validate assumptions, and sharpen priorities with a consultant who knows the domain.

4

Get your results

A prioritized use case report, architecture concept, and concrete next steps - ready to share with your leadership or technical team.
