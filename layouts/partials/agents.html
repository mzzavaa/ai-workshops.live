What the Agents Do

Six specialized analysis engines

Each agent is purpose-built for a specific part of the discovery process, running in parallel to deliver comprehensive analysis fast.

Use case discovery and scoring

Identifies relevant AI opportunities from your business context and scores each by impact, feasibility, and strategic fit.

Technical feasibility analysis

Evaluates data readiness, integration complexity, and team capability requirements for each identified use case.

AWS cost estimation

Models expected AWS infrastructure costs at scale, including compute, storage, and managed AI service pricing for your use cases.

Architecture recommendations

Proposes high-level solution architectures based on your stack, team, and compliance requirements - matched to AWS best practices.

Risk assessment

Surfaces technical, organisational, and compliance risks for each approach, with suggested mitigations ranked by likelihood.

Implementation roadmap generation

Produces a phased delivery plan with milestones, dependencies, and decision gates - scoped to your team size and constraints.
